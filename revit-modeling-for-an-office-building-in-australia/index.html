Tolerance to scan ±15 mm (±0.6 in)

Definitions

  • Point cloud to Revit conversion: building native Revit elements that follow measured scan points.
  • LOD 300: elements are modeled with accurate size, shape, location and orientation.
  • Registration error: misalignment between scan positions that can shift elements sideways from one floor to the next.
  • Split-level floor: a slab that steps in height within one level, so each change needs its own measured section.

Method: curved facade

  1. Slice the point cloud into thin horizontal bands at several heights.
  2. Fit a reference circle to each band.
  3. Average the center and radius over three or four bands to cancel scan noise.
  4. Model the wall from that single arc instead of short straight segments.
  5. Build a custom window family with adjustable radius and rotation, then set it flush against the wall curve.

Method: basements and structure

  1. Cut detailed sections through stepped and split-level areas and read each height from the cloud.
  2. Model floor slabs first as reference masses.
  3. Split the work by basement level and smaller zones.
  4. Isolate each column-to-beam and wall-to-beam junction with 3D section views and site photos.
  5. Model real openings and gaps, then check that vertical elements align floor to floor.

Known difficulties

Issue Effect Response
Curved facade True edge levels and wall curve are hard to read Banded circle fitting
Curved windows Standard families do not match wall lean Custom parametric window
Dense junctions Real connections are hard to read Section views plus photos
Wide basements Floor points are hard to match over a large area Work by level and zone

Outcome

The model gives design teams measured existing conditions and gives facility teams a record of structure and curved elements for maintenance planning. Services covered: architecture scan to BIM, structural scan to BIM and as-built BIM modeling.
