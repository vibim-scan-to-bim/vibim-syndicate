Scan to BIM for Texas Projects: A Technical Reference

This reference sums up how ViBIM scopes and delivers Scan to BIM work for Texas buildings, from the data a team supplies to the files it receives back. It is written for BIM coordinators and project engineers who need to brief an outsourcing partner without a long email thread.

Definitions used in this workflow

  • Point cloud: a set of measured 3D points captured by laser scanning, used as the geometric reference for modeling.
  • Scan to BIM: the process of tracing building elements from a point cloud into an intelligent Revit model.
  • LOD (Level of Development): the agreed degree of geometric and data detail for each model element.
  • LOI (Level of Information): the non-geometric data attached to elements, validated together with LOD.
  • Tolerance: the allowed deviation between the model and the scan. ViBIM works to ±3-5mm across disciplines.

Input checklist

  1. Registered point cloud, with RCP or RCS preferred and E57 accepted. Keep single files under 5GB.
  2. TrueView or site photos to confirm materials and finishes.
  3. Project scope: required LOD, disciplines and deliverable formats.
  4. Existing CAD or PDF drawings to resolve hidden elements and match grid lines.
  5. BIM standards: naming conventions, templates and compliance rules.
  6. Timeline with deadlines and milestones.
  7. A named contact for technical queries and QC markups.

If no point cloud exists yet, a preliminary estimate can be prepared from TrueView files, map links or site photographs.

Workflow outline

The team follows a thirteen-step sequence. The early steps prepare the ground: brief analysis, point cloud preparation with coordinate setup, then levels and grids. Modeling follows by discipline, with architecture first and structure and MEP when required. The middle steps check the work through LOD and LOI validation, family creation, coordination and clash checks. The closing steps cover QA/QC against tolerance, a first model submission, revisions from the client's QC markups, and final delivery.

Deliverables

  • Revit models to the agreed LOD
  • 2D floor plans, sections and elevations generated from the model
  • An accuracy check that compares the model to the original scan
  • Export formats: RVT, IFC, DWG and NWC
